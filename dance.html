💃 Dance

What 7th graders study

Middle school dance focuses on building technique, rhythm, coordination, flexibility, and strength while exploring different styles such as jazz, hip-hop, ballet, modern, and cultural dances. Students learn dance vocabulary, practice choreography, create their own movement, and learn to perform with confidence and give respectful feedback to others.
