🧮 Math Helper

Type a math expression and press Solve (or Enter). Works with fractions, mixed numbers (2 1/4), decimals, negatives, x or *, ÷ or /, and ^ for powers. To convert a decimal, just type it (like 0.45) to see it as a fraction too.

Note: this solves math expressions, not word problems. Turn the word problem into an expression first!
