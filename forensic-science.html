🕵️ Forensic Science

What 7th graders study

Forensic science uses science to solve mysteries and investigate evidence. Middle school introductions usually cover observation skills, fingerprint patterns, handwriting and document analysis, simple chemistry tests, hair and fiber comparison, and how investigators use logic and evidence to reach conclusions, all while connecting to biology, chemistry, and critical thinking.
