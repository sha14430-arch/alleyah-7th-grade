🔒 How safe is this?

  • This is light protection, not bank-grade security. It stops classmates from casually using your profile.
  • Profiles are stored on a free public test service (restful-api.dev). Anyone who knows how could read, change or delete the records, and a 4 to 6 digit PIN can be guessed by a determined person with a computer.
  • Your PIN itself is never sent: only a scrambled (salted PBKDF2-SHA-256) check of it. Saved data is just your username, display name, grade, test scores, points and avatar.
  • So: use a nickname, don't reuse a PIN from anything important, and never put private information here.
  • The free service allows about 50 requests a day per network. The site saves on this device first and syncs only when needed, so if the limit is reached nothing is lost; it syncs later.
  • You can read every page without logging in. Logging in is only needed to save scores, points and avatar items.
