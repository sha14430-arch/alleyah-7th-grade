🎨 Drawing & Painting

What 7th graders study

In middle school drawing and painting, students practice the elements of art (line, shape, form, color, value, texture, and space) and principles of design such as balance, contrast, and emphasis. Common projects include observational drawing, shading, perspective, color mixing, and working with materials like pencil, charcoal, watercolor, and acrylic paint.
