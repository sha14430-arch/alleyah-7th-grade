🌍 Social Studies

What 7th graders study

Seventh grade social studies commonly covers world history and geography, such as ancient and medieval civilizations, or, in some states, state and early U.S. history. Students learn how geography, government, economics, and culture shape societies, and they practice reading maps, analyzing primary sources, and explaining cause and effect.
