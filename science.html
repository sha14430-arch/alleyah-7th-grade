🔬 Science

What 7th graders study

In 7th grade science, students often explore life science topics such as cells and how they work, body systems, genetics and heredity, and how living things interact in ecosystems. Many classes also practice the scientific method: asking questions, making hypotheses, running experiments, collecting data, and drawing conclusions from evidence.
