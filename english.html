What 7th graders study

In 7th grade English Language Arts, students read fiction, nonfiction, poetry, and drama, and learn to identify themes, analyze characters, and cite text evidence to support their ideas. They also write narratives, informational pieces, and arguments, while building vocabulary, grammar skills, and confidence in discussions and presentations.
